# Data Deletion Instructions for CraftKart

**Last updated:** 2026-10-28

CraftKart is an offline-first app. All data is stored locally on your device only - no account, no cloud sync, no remote data storage.

## How to delete your data
You can delete all CraftKart data in one of the following ways:

1. **Uninstall the app** (recommended) - Uninstalling CraftKart from your Android device will remove the app and all locally stored data including products, sales, customers, receipts, and images.
2. **Clear app data** - On Android: Settings → Apps → CraftKart → Storage → Clear Data. This deletes all local data while keeping the app installed.

## Notes
- We cannot delete data remotely because we do not store any of your data on our servers.
- If you have backed up your device, restoring a backup may restore deleted data.
