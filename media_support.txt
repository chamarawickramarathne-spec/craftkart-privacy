CraftKart - Handmade Shop Inventory & POS

2. SHORT DESCRIPTION
============================================================
CraftKart is a simple inventory + point-of-sale app for handmade
businesses. Track your products and customers, sell with cash, card,
UPI or credit, and share PDF receipts - all offline on your phone.

============================================================
3. LONG DESCRIPTION
============================================================
CraftKart is built for small handmade businesses. Manage your full
product catalog with photos, organize items into categories, and
keep a live eye on stock levels with automatic low-stock alerts.
Scan barcodes and QR codes with your camera to add items to a sale
or file a new product in seconds, and adjust stock levels with one
tap - add or remove quantities with a reason logged automatically.
Selling is fast and friendly: search or browse items, tap to add to
cart, apply a discount, pick cash, card, UPI or credit for a regular
customer, and complete the sale. Stock is updated automatically and
every sale is saved to your sales history with a shareable PDF
receipt. Know your numbers with reports: today, this week, this month
or all time - revenue, orders, average sale and profit. Keep your
customers on file with credit balances, loyalty points and group
discounts, and issue refunds that restore stock in one tap.
Everything is stored safely on your phone - no internet needed.
Works in English and Sinhala. Perfect for greeting cards, bookmarks,
gift tags and any handcrafted product.
