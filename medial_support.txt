2. SHORT DESCRIPTION
============================================================
CraftKart is a simple inventory + point-of-sale app for handmade
businesses. Track products and customers, sell with cash, card, UPI or
credit, and share PDF receipts - all offline on your phone.

============================================================
3. LONG DESCRIPTION
============================================================
CraftKart is built for small handmade businesses. Manage your full
product catalog with photos, organize items into categories, and keep a
live eye on stock levels with automatic low-stock alerts. Scan barcodes
and QR codes with your camera to add items to a sale or file a new
product in seconds, and adjust stock levels with one tap - add or remove
quantities with a reason logged automatically.

Selling is fast and friendly: search or browse items, tap to add to cart,
apply a discount, pick cash, card, UPI or credit for a regular customer,
and complete the sale. Stock updates automatically and every sale is
saved to your sales history with a shareable PDF receipt.

Reports show today, this week, this month, last 30 days or all-time
revenue, orders, average sale and profit, plus top sellers and a category
breakdown. Keep customers on file with credit balances, loyalty points
and group discounts, and issue refunds that restore stock in one tap.

Everything is stored safely on your phone - no internet needed. Works in
English and Sinhala. Perfect for greeting cards, bookmarks, gift tags and
any handcrafted product.

Who it is for: crafters, home-based makers, gift shops and small stalls
that sell handmade goods without a full retail system.
